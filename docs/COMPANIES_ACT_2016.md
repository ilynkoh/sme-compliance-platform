# Companies Act 2016 Compliance Reference

## Overview

This document outlines the key compliance requirements under the Malaysian Companies Act 2016 that are checked by the SME Compliance Platform.

## 1. Financial Statement Requirements

### Section 375-399: Directors' Responsibilities

- Directors must ensure company keeps accounting records
- Financial statements must present true and fair view
- Must comply with Malaysian Financial Reporting Standards (MFRS)
- Annual financial statements must be approved and signed

### Schedule 2: MFRS Compliance

Small companies may use simplified financial reporting standards if they meet the definition of a small company:
- Revenue < RM50 million
- Total assets < RM25 million
- Employees < 50

## 2. Balance Sheet Requirements

### Mandatory Classification

**Assets:**
- Current assets (expected to be realized within 12 months)
- Non-current assets (long-term investments)

**Liabilities:**
- Current liabilities (due within 12 months)
- Non-current liabilities (long-term obligations)

**Equity:**
- Share capital
- Reserves (accumulated profits, revaluation reserves)
- Retained earnings

### Key Principles

1. **Historical Cost vs Fair Value**: Assets generally valued at:
   - Historical cost (most common)
   - Fair value (for certain investments)

2. **Conservatism**: Uncertainties resolved conservatively

3. **Matching Principle**: Expenses matched to revenue period

## 3. Audit Requirements

### Section 379: Audit Exemption Threshold

Companies are exempt from audit if:
- Annual revenue < RM35 million (currently)
- AND total assets < RM21 million (at end of financial year)
- AND average employees < 50

### Section 374: Appointment of Auditors

- First auditor appointed by directors
- Subsequent auditors appointed by members in general meeting
- Auditor must be a qualified member of MIA (Malaysian Institute of Accountants)

## 4. Disclosure Requirements

### Related Party Transactions (MFRS 124)

**Must Disclose:**
- Names of related parties
- Nature of relationship
- Transaction amounts and terms
- Balances outstanding at year-end

**Related Parties Include:**
- Directors and their families
- Key management personnel
- Companies under common control
- Associated companies

### Contingent Liabilities (MFRS 137)

**Must Disclose if Material:**
- Legal proceedings
- Warranty obligations
- Loan guarantees
- Environmental obligations

### Subsequent Events (MFRS 110)

**Adjusting Events** (adjust statements):
- Bankruptcy of customer
- Court judgment confirming liability
- Inventory obsolescence discovered

**Non-adjusting Events** (disclose only):
- Major acquisition or disposal
- Issue of shares or bonds
- Significant fire/flood

## 5. Specific Compliance Checks

### The Platform Validates:

1. **Trial Balance Balance**
   - Total debits = Total credits
   - Reference: Fundamental accounting principle

2. **Account Classification**
   - Assets, liabilities, equity properly segregated
   - Current vs non-current properly distinguished
   - Reference: Schedule 2, MFRS 101

3. **Materiality Assessment**
   - Individual and aggregate materiality evaluated
   - Reference: MFRS Conceptual Framework

4. **Completeness**
   - All required accounts present
   - No unusual account balances
   - Reference: MFRS 101, 102

5. **Disclosure Quality**
   - Related parties disclosed
   - Contingencies identified
   - Going concern assessment
   - Reference: MFRS 124, 137, 570

## 6. Filing Requirements

### Annual Filing

**Section 395:** All companies must file:
- Financial statements
- Directors' report
- Auditors' report (if applicable)
- Within 4 months of financial year-end

### Form: Annual Return (AA1/AA3)

- Company details
- Directors and officers
- Members list
- Share capital

## 7. Penalties for Non-Compliance

### Administrative Penalties
- Late filing fees: RM300-RM3,000
- Removal from registry

### Criminal Penalties
- Falsifying records: Fine up to RM250,000 + imprisonment
- Fraudulent statements: Fine up to RM500,000 + imprisonment

## 9. Best Practices for SMEs

### Record Keeping
1. Maintain comprehensive accounting records
2. Keep supporting documents (invoices, receipts)
3. Use accounting software
4. Monthly reconciliations

### Internal Controls
1. Segregation of duties
2. Authorization procedures
3. Regular reconciliations
4. Periodic management accounts

### Financial Reporting
1. Regular review of financial position
2. Monitor compliance requirements
3. Timely filing of documents
4. Professional advice when needed
